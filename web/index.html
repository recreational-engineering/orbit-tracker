View mode M

Orbit view: object fixed at the centre, flying up; the orbit is a vertical line and the Earth turns below. Ground view: Earth fixed, north up, the object passes by. Flat earth: azimuthal equidistant map around the north pole showing the whole Earth. Pilot view: looking prograde from the object; the Earth turns below up to the horizon. Tap the bottom readout on the display for the view menu. Drag to look around; the view glides back after 4 s, or at once when you tap the time at the top.
